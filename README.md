A small program that writes exactly one emoji to `/output/entry.txt`: 😇 (U+1F607, Smiling Face with Halo) — a gentle, kind smile under a halo, the one emoji most likely to make a stranger smile.

## Why this emoji

- **Warm at first glance:** a face that smiles straight at the reader — kind, friendly, no context needed.
- **Universal:** a stranger of any age or background reads a halo smile the same way, with no irony or in-joke that could land wrong.
- **Chosen, not default:** not the first smiley that comes to mind — the halo makes the pick deliberate while staying instantly readable.
- **Right for a stranger:** a kind smile carries no intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation. A hug or heart-hands from someone unknown can feel too intimate; a plain smiley is the default. The halo smile is warm without overstepping.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

No network, no model calls, deterministic output.
